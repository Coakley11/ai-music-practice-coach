**What to do**

**4 min** — isolated pattern: Isolate the strumming pattern on open strings or one chord before adding changes.

**5 min** — subdivision & accents: Use muting and accents to define the pattern.

**5 min** — chord changes without stopping: Watch chord-transition gaps that stop the groove.

**4 min** — song / backing application: Apply this on **Shape of You**. Keep the pattern through the form.

**2 min** — review: Play one short take and notice where the groove breaks.

**What to listen for**

- The strumming or groove hand keeps moving through changes

- Subdivision stays even; accents land where you intended

- Chord-transition gaps do not stop the pattern

**When to progress**

- Move to the next block only when the current one stays clean on most repetitions.

- Add upstrokes, accents, or tempo only after the hand stays continuous.
