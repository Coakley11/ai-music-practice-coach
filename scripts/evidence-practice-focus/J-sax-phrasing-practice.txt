# Practice page coaching — Saxophone / Phrasing

## Recommended drills
- Play 2-bar and 4-bar phrases with a clear ending. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Leave space after each phrase instead of filling every beat. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Answer a short idea with a related but varied reply. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).

## What to listen / watch for
- Phrases have a beginning, direction, and ending
- There is audible space / breath between ideas
- Dynamics help the sentence, not random volume
