# H-ami-sax-tone.txt
Instrument: Saxophone
Practice Focus: Tone
Question: What should I practice today?
Intent: CoachIntent.PRACTICE_PLAN

**Today's priority:** Saxophone tone in **Shape of You**.

**30-minute Saxophone tone session**

**Today's goal:** produce a centered, stable **Saxophone** tone that stays consistent through sustained notes, articulation, and actual music.

**Recommendation:** If tone stayed centered through long tones **and** your phrase, you met today's goal.

**What to do**

### 1. 8 min — Centered long tones

- Start in a comfortable **middle register**.
- Hold each note about **8–10 seconds** at a medium-soft dynamic.
- Keep the air steady through the entire note; rest briefly between reps.
- Move chromatically or through a simple scale across **5–7 notes**.

**Listen for:** clean start, centered core, stable pitch, even volume, minimal air noise.

**Ready when:** **4 of 5** notes begin cleanly and stay stable for 10 seconds.

### 2. 6 min — Tone through register changes

- Play **slow 4–6 note slurred fragments** (scale or simple interval).
- Match tone color when crossing register boundaries.
- Repeat each fragment until low/middle/high feel connected.

**Listen for:** no sudden thinning or spreading of sound between registers.

**Ready when:** you can play the fragment **3 times** with no obvious tone drop between registers.

### 3. 5 min — Clear attacks without losing tone

- On one comfortable note: **slur 4**, then **tongue 4** at the same dynamic.
- Keep the tone centered immediately after the tongue releases.
- Expand to a short **2–3 note pattern** if stable.

**Listen for:** attacks that are clear but not explosive or breathy.

**Ready when:** **8 of 10** attacks are clean without a thin or airy start.

### 4. 8 min — Musical application

- Apply the same tone work to a short phrase from "Shape of You".
- Play **very slowly**; preserve the centered sound from your long-tone work.
- Choose a phrase that moves through more than one register if possible.

**Listen for:** tone quality stays as good in the phrase as on isolated notes.

**Ready when:** the phrase stays centered **3 times in a row** at a slow tempo.

### 5. 3 min — Review

- Replay the **hardest note or phrase** from the session.
- Optional: record one short take.
- Name **one specific tone issue** to target next time.

**At the end:** judge whether your sound has a clearer core and steadier air than when you started.

**What to listen for**

- Focused core rather than airy or diffuse sound

- Stable pitch and steady air through the note

- Even dynamic from start to finish

**When to progress**

- Advance tempo or range only when the current block stays clean on most repetitions.
