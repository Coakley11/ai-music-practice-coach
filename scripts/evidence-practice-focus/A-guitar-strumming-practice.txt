# Practice page coaching — Guitar / Strumming

## Recommended drills
- Keep the strumming or picking hand moving. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Isolate the strumming pattern on open strings or one chord before adding changes. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Practice downstrokes first, then add upstrokes on a steady eighth-note grid. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).

## What to listen / watch for
- The strumming or groove hand keeps moving through changes
- Subdivision stays even; accents land where you intended
- Chord-transition gaps do not stop the pattern
