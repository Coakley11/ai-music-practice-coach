# Practice page coaching — Saxophone / Articulation

## Recommended drills
- Tonguing syllables (tu/du), clean starts, and even slurring. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Isolate attacks on long tones, then on a simple melody. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Alternate legato and staccato on the same pitches. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).

## What to listen / watch for
- Each attack speaks cleanly and on time
- Legato and separated notes are clearly different
- Tone does not thin when articulation gets busier
