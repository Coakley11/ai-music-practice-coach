# Practice page coaching — Saxophone / Tone

## Recommended drills
- Emphasize air support, stable embouchure, resonance, and register consistency. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Start with long tones: stable pitch, even air or contact, no pinched sound. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Match tone quality across registers before adding speed. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).

## What to listen / watch for
- Centered, stable sound without pinching or collapsing
- Register matches — same quality high and low
- Attacks and releases stay consistent
