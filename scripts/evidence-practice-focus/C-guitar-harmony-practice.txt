# Practice page coaching — Guitar / Harmony

## Recommended drills
- Spell the chord tones of each harmony in the section. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Practice 3rds and 7ths as a slow guide-tone line through the changes. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Connect adjacent chords with the smallest voice-leading motion. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).

## What to listen / watch for
- Chord tones land on strong beats
- Guide tones (3rds/7ths) connect smoothly
- You can name what the next chord is asking for
