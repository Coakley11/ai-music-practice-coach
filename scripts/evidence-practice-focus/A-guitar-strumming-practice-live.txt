PERSONALIZED COACH EXERCISE
Conservatory Coach Plan 1: Intro

Song: Perfect
Target section: Intro — 7 bars
Today: 30 minutes on Guitar, Intermediate, Strumming
Chord focus: A → E/G#
Loop: A | E/G# | F#m7 | E | Dadd9 | E | A
Section character: make the entrance steady and uncluttered

1. Technical Warm-up (5 min)

Play/sing the chord tones of A: A - C# - E. Then resolve into E/G# with steady groove and connected phrasing.
Aim for: Subdivision stays even; accents land where you intended

2. Song-Specific Drill (11 min)

Keep the strumming or picking hand moving. Use A → E/G# in Intro (A | E/G# | F#m7 | E | Dadd9 | E | A).

3. Instrument + Focus Coaching (9 min)

Strumming drill (6/8 pulse): loop A | E/G# | F#m7 | E | Dadd9 | E | A for 3 passes. Count 1-2-3 4-5-6; feel two big beats per bar. Accent beat 1 and beat 4; keep the inner eighths flowing. Pattern: D - U D - U or arpeggiate bass-treble-treble twice per bar. Pass 1 is muted strings only; pass 2 adds chord changes; pass 3 follows the section note: Keep the groove consistent and make phrase endings clear.
Isolate the strumming pattern on open strings or one chord before adding changes. Use A → E/G# in Intro (A | E/G# | F#m7 | E | Dadd9 | E | A).

4. Creativity / Musicianship

Create one alternate version of the same 7-bar phrase: new register, new voicing, new articulation, or a small fill into E/G#.

5. Progress Check (5 min)

Connect the drill to the backing track for 9 minutes, then record one full pass of Intro.
Success standard: The strumming or groove hand keeps moving through changes
