# Practice page coaching — Guitar / Timing

## Recommended drills
- Practice with a metronome on every beat, then on 2 and 4 only. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Speak or tap the subdivision before playing it. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).
- Loop a short passage and listen for rushing into chord changes or phrase peaks. Use **Em -> C** in **Verse** (Em | C | G | D).

## What to listen / watch for
- Beat 1 is obvious and unhurried
- Subdivision stays even; watch rushing into peaks or changes
- Rests still sit on the grid
