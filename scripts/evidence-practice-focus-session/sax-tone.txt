Session (~30 min) for **Saxophone** with Practice Focus **Tone** (tone).

Blocks:
- 8 min long tones: Start with long tones: stable pitch, even air or contact, no pinched sound.
- 7 min register consistency: Emphasize air support, stable embouchure, resonance, and register consistency.
- 5 min dynamics & attacks: Practice controlled crescendos and decrescendos on sustained notes.
- 7 min repertoire application: Apply this on **Demo Song**. Keep the same tone target in the music.
- 3 min review: Name one tone win and one register or attack still to stabilize.

Listen for: Centered, stable sound without pinching or collapsing; Register matches — same quality high and low; Attacks and releases stay consistent
