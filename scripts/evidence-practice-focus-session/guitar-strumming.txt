Session (~30 min) for **Guitar** with Practice Focus **Strumming** (rhythm groove).

Blocks:
- 5 min isolated pattern: Isolate the strumming pattern on open strings or one chord before adding changes.
- 7 min subdivision & accents: Use muting and accents to define the pattern.
- 7 min chord changes without stopping: Watch chord-transition gaps that stop the groove.
- 7 min song / backing application: Apply this on **Demo Song**. Keep the pattern through the form.
- 4 min review: Play one short take and notice where the groove breaks.

Listen for: The strumming or groove hand keeps moving through changes; Subdivision stays even; accents land where you intended; Chord-transition gaps do not stop the pattern
