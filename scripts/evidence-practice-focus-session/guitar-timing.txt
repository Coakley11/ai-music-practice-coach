Session (~30 min) for **Guitar** with Practice Focus **Timing** (timing).

Blocks:
- 9 min metronome groove: Practice with a metronome on every beat, then on 2 and 4 only.
- 8 min subdivision placement: Speak or tap the subdivision before playing it.
- 8 min section loop: Apply this on **Demo Song**. Loop a short passage until placement stays reliable.
- 5 min review: Notice where time wobbles — usually into peaks or chord changes.

Listen for: Beat 1 is obvious and unhurried; Subdivision stays even; watch rushing into peaks or changes; Rests still sit on the grid
