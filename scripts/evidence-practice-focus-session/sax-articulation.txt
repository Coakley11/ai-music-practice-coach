Session (~30 min) for **Saxophone** with Practice Focus **Articulation** (articulation).

Blocks:
- 4 min warm-up tone centering: Play a few comfortable notes on **Saxophone** with steady sound before adding articulation.
- 9 min single-note tonguing: Isolate attacks on long tones, then on a simple melody.
- 8 min slur/tongue contrast: Alternate legato and staccato on the same pitches.
- 6 min musical phrase application: Apply this on **Demo Song**. Use one articulation pattern through the phrase.
- 3 min review: Check that entrances speak on time without thinning the sound.

Listen for: Each attack speaks cleanly and on time; Legato and separated notes are clearly different; Tone does not thin when articulation gets busier
