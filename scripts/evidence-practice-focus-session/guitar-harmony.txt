Session (~30 min) for **Guitar** with Practice Focus **Harmony** (harmony).

Blocks:
- 8 min chord tones: Spell the chord tones of each harmony in the section.
- 10 min guide tones / voice leading: Practice 3rds and 7ths as a slow guide-tone line through the changes.
- 8 min section application: Apply this on **Demo Song**. Pause on each new chord and resolve to a chord tone.
- 4 min review: Name the function of the hardest change and whether the line resolved.

Listen for: Chord tones land on strong beats; Guide tones (3rds/7ths) connect smoothly; You can name what the next chord is asking for
