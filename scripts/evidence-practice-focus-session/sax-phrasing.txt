Session (~30 min) for **Saxophone** with Practice Focus **Phrasing** (phrasing).

Blocks:
- 7 min phrase length: Play 2-bar and 4-bar phrases with a clear ending.
- 8 min breathing / space: Leave space after each phrase instead of filling every beat.
- 8 min question and answer: Answer a short idea with a related but varied reply.
- 4 min song application: Apply this on **Demo Song**.
- 3 min review: Listen for sentence shape: start, direction, and resolution.

Listen for: Phrases have a beginning, direction, and ending; There is audible space / breath between ideas; Dynamics help the sentence, not random volume
