# Creative Arrangement Assistant - Demo Song
**Target style:** Pop Rock | **Instrument:** Guitar | **Level:** Intermediate | **Focus:** Melody

## Producer Direction
- **Overall feel:** driving 8ths, bigger chorus lift, sustained power texture.
- Keep the original form recognizable; change density, register, rhythm, and voicing before rewriting the whole harmony.
- Add one arrangement device per section: register, rhythm, voicing color, or density.

## Practice Focus suggestions (advisory only)
_These bias what to notice — they do not rewrite your arrangement unless you choose to apply a change._
- **Melody:** keep one clear melodic foreground; demote competing lines.
- Use doubling or a quiet countermelody only where it supports the hook.
- Separate registers so the **Guitar** melody stays readable.

## Focused Section: Verse
### Verse - 4 bars
- **Musical job:** I-V-vi-IV pop loop.
- **Arrangement move:** palm-muted pulse or arpeggiated open voicings.
- **Guitar part:** Use a capo/open-string color if it fits the key; fingerpick **G -> D** with bass on beat 1 and upper strings on the offbeats.

## Keep It Musical
- Preserve the original song identity: keep the strongest hook, phrase shape, or bass motion recognizable.
- Make contrast audible between verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, and outro; do not make every section equally dense.
- Practice Focus recommendations stay suggestions until you explicitly apply a change.
