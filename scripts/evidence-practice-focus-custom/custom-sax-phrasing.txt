## Practice Focus drills — **Phrasing**
**Instrument:** Saxophone  
**Progression:** G · D · Em · C  

- Play 2-bar and 4-bar phrases with a clear ending. Use **G -> D** in **custom progression** (G · D · Em · C).
- Leave space after each phrase instead of filling every beat. Use **G -> D** in **custom progression** (G · D · Em · C).
- Answer a short idea with a related but varied reply. Use **G -> D** in **custom progression** (G · D · Em · C).
- Play 2 bars / rest 2 bars over **G · D · Em · C**.
- Question-and-answer: short idea, then a varied reply that resolves.
- Leave audible space; shape contour toward a clear ending.
- Match the **Pop** feel only after the Focus goal stays clean.

**Listen for:** Phrases have a beginning, direction, and ending; There is audible space / breath between ideas; Dynamics help the sentence, not random volume
