💡 Quick coaching — Intro

Speak the lyric in rhythm before singing. Mark breaths, keep vowels forward, and stay softer in verses until the chorus asks for more. Plan your inhale points between phrases. Take low, diaphragmatic breaths and keep the air column steady through sustained notes. Set tempo and mood; don't overplay. Hold the suspended chord for a moment to build tension, then let it resolve smoothly into the next chord.
