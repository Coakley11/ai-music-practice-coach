Melody

Shape phrases in 17-bar sections that land on the 3rd of each new chord as the harmony changes, keeping the bossa nova pulse. The Generated Practice Melody on the Practice page (Chart & Melody) offers more ideas over these changes (generated for this song's chords, not the original melody).

🔍
Your focus: Breath Control

Plan breaths at the ends of phrases so the 17-bar sections flow without breaking a line.

🔥
Your challenge

Sing the verse at 95 BPM breathing only every 4 bars, keeping the sound steady to the end of each phrase.
